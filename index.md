# RA4HGN

This site is dedicated to the practical use of **GNU Radio on microwave bands** (13 cm, 6 cm, 3 cm and above) for amateur radio applications.

Here I collect and publish:
*   **SDR experiments on microwaves** — PlutoSDR, USRP and other platforms.
*   **GNU Radio flowgraphs** for reception and transmission on microwave bands.
*   **Measurement techniques** — noise figure and gain of LNAs, power, spectrum.
*   **Articles and notes** on what works, what doesn't, and why.

The goal of this site is to document my own experience and make it useful to other amateur radio operators who want to work with GNU Radio on microwaves.
